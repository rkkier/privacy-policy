Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how our mobile application (the "Application") handles user data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure user experience.

By using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our Application is designed with a "local-first" approach. We do not operate a remote database, and your personal information is stored strictly on your own device.

A. Authentication Data (Android Credential Manager)

To allow you to log in securely, the Application integrates the Android Credential Manager API using Google Sign-In. When you authenticate, the Application accesses the following information provided by your Google Account:

  • User ID: A unique identifier linked to your Google Account.
  • Email Address: Your primary Google account email.
  • Name & Profile Picture: Your public Google profile name and display picture.

How we use and store this data: This information is used solely to create your local user profile and manage your account state within the app. This data is stored 100% locally on your device (via secure local storage) and is never transmitted to our servers or any third-party databases.

B. Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

The Application integrates Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google Inc., to display advertisements.

To serve relevant ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device information directly from the Application. This data includes:

  • IP addresses.
  • Device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID or Android ID).
  • Mobile carrier and general location data (country/city level).
  • App interaction data (ad views, clicks, and launch frequency).

2. Data Storage and Security

  • Personal Data: All personal data obtained via the Credential Manager remains on your physical device. If you delete the Application or clear its cache/data from your device settings, this information is permanently erased.
  • Security: Because your personal data never leaves your device, it is secured by your device's built-in Android operating system security measures. We do not transmit or store this data over the internet.

3. Third-Party Services and Privacy Links

While we do not share your personal account information with anyone, the third-party ad network integrated into our app has its own privacy policies governing how they use your device data:

  • Google AdMob & Partner Policies: You can learn more about how Google uses data when you use our app by visiting How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our Application does not knowingly collect or store personal data from children under the age of 13. Because all account authentication data is processed locally and ad delivery is governed by Google AdMob, we encourage parents to monitor their children's device settings regarding personalized advertising.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.
